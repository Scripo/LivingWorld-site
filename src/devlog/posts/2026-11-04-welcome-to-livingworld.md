Hello, and welcome to the first LivingWorld devlog.

LivingWorld is a fantasy world simulation where you play as an **ancestral spirit** guiding one band of people across generations. You don’t control the whole world, or even everyone in your band. You guide its leaders, and they bring you their plans. You can approve, change or decline them, or simply trust them to handle things.

## The idea

I’ve always loved games where the world feels like it would keep going without you. Dwarf Fortress’s histories, the scale of Songs of Syx, the family drama of Crusader Kings. LivingWorld tries to combine some of that on a personal scale. One small band, in a generated world full of other bands that live by the same rules.

Your people might stay a small wandering band. They might become famous crafters, settle a river valley, or split in two after a hard winter. When a split happens, you choose which group to follow, and the other one carries on without you.

## What already works

Most of the work so far is in the simulation itself, the part you can’t see in a screenshot:

- A generated world that advances one day at a time, through seasons and years.
- Bands of Humans, Dwarves, Elves and Goblins that gather, hunt, keep herds, migrate and sometimes split apart.
- Leaders, scouts and crafters who grow out of each band’s actual members, not out of thin air.
- Scouts who travel out and come back with reports of what they saw, sometimes unsure of it.
- **Craft Circles**, small groups of crafters who make tools, train newcomers and keep their knowledge alive when a master dies.
- A playable prototype with a world map. You choose your people, band and leader on Day 0, then answer your leader’s proposals as days pass.

## Where it’s heading

The images on this site are **concept art**. They show the look I’m aiming for: a living 3D camp as the home screen, a map where the unknown fades into mist, and leaders who come to you with real decisions. The current prototype is much plainer, and I’ll show real footage here as it improves.

Next up is deepening crafts and knowledge, so a band’s discoveries really change how it lives, and eventually the first steps toward permanent settlements.

## What to expect here

I’ll post updates when there’s something real to show: new systems, design decisions, and the occasional strange story the simulation tells all by itself. Short videos will go on the [Videos](/videos/) page too.
