## The richest clan

This world started with twenty clans, five each of Humans, Dwarves, Elves and Goblins. One of them, the elves of **Clan Sylvaren**, began with 17 people.

Four years later they had grown to 39 and held the biggest food stores in the world: 390, against 251 for the next-best clan. On paper they were winning.

## The fifth winter

Then winter came, with 41 elves to feed. In 80 days they lost 73% of their food.

And the strange part: all through that winter, their scout kept reporting good food on the land right next to camp.

## The split

On day 1540, a curious elf named **Ylena** walked away with 14 followers to found a clan of her own.

Nobody scripted that. When a group splits off, the simulation records why, and for Ylena it recorded **resource pressure**, together with the scout's reports of the land nearby. Shrinking stores at home, good news from the field: that was enough for her to go.

## What happened next

Nine months later, Ylena's new clan is running out of food too. Whether leaving was brave or a mistake is the question for part 2.

This is what I'm building LivingWorld for: stories like this one aren't written by me, they come out of the rules. Every clan follows the same ones, and sometimes the result is a rise and a fall like Sylvaren's.

## Also this week

Under the hood, I replaced a tangle of special-case equipment bonuses with one shared stock of **Tools** for each band. They make it, use it for gathering and crafting, and wear it out over time. You won't see it in the video, but it makes the economy simpler and easier to follow.
